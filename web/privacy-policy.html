Privacy Policy

At 7Pay Services, we value your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our platform for home services.

1) Information We Collect

Personal Identity: Name, contact number, and email address.

Service Address: Physical address for providing on-demand services (cleaning, plumbing, etc.).

Payment Information: Billing details processed via secure third-party gateways.

Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and device information for platform optimization.

2) Purpose of Data Collection

We use your information to:

• Book and schedule your requested home services.
• Process payments and issue invoices.
• Send service updates and promotional offers (with your consent).
• Improve our service quality and customer support.

3) Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share necessary details (name, phone, address) with our verified service professionals (plumbers, electricians, etc.) to fulfill your booking.

4) Your Rights (DPDPA Compliance)

Under Indian law, you have the right to:

Access your data and request a summary of how it is used.
Correct or update any inaccurate information.
Withdraw Consent at any time.
Erasure: Request that we delete your personal data (subject to legal record-keeping requirements).
